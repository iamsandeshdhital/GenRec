Key Features

  • Input Verbalization
  • Context Engineering
  • Reward Integration
  • Prefill-Only Serving
  • Two-Phase Training

Results

From the paper's large-scale A/B test:

  • Statistically significant gains
  • Fewer training examples needed
  • Reduced input signals
  • Improved offline & online metrics

Architecture

Phase 1: Domain Adaptation

Adapt open-source LLM to Netflix data, developing deep understanding of catalog and member behavior.

Phase 2: Ranking Post-Training

Post-train with recommendation-ranking specific data, labels, and reward signals.

Serving: Prefill-Only Inference

Cost-constrained serving design based on prefill-only inference approach.
